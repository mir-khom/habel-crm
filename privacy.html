English

Habel CRM is an internal service of "Nobel Edu". Using YouTube API Services, it reads only public comments on our official YouTube channel (comment text, the author's public YouTube display name, date, video title) and posts replies on behalf of the channel.

  • This data is stored in the company CRM solely to contact people who show interest in our courses.
  • We do not sell or share this data with third parties.
  • Google account access is granted only by the channel owner and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To request deletion of your data: +998 55 588 88 81 or Telegram @habeledumenejer.
